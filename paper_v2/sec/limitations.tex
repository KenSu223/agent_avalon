\section*{Limitations}

The guess rate is specific to a reader model and decoding; \pcme{} must be re-run when either changes. The resistant and
answerable strata are not randomly assigned and differ in difficulty, which the oracle quantifies but cannot remove;
the minimal pairs address this only for our own items. LoCoMo evidence annotations are noisy and conversations repeat
information, so our evidence ablations are approximate. The same LLM judge scores both closed-book and memory-based
answers, so its leniency affects $g$ and $Y$ alike but does not necessarily cancel; the 150-item human validation of the
judges and the 100-item human review of the new items are pending and no human results are reported here. Our harness
compacts more often than deployed products do; we report a realistic-trigger robustness check, but absolute retention
values for specific products should not be read as product evaluations. MemoryAgentBench subsets have 100 items, which
limits power for pairwise comparisons; we therefore report only statistically confirmed rank changes. All items are in
English and set in one fictional workplace, and they were written by a single LLM, which likely makes them stylistically
homogeneous. Preference-following benchmarks have no closed-book analogue of a guess rate and are only described.

\section*{Ethics statement}
The study uses public benchmarks under their licences (LoCoMo and PrefEval are CC BY-NC 4.0 and are not redistributed)
and a fictional setting with no personal data. Items deliberately state false general facts as local rules; they are
intended only for evaluating memory and are labelled as such in the datasheet.
