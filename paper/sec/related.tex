\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Degradation in agent memory.} \citet{zhang2026faulty} measure that continual
consolidation makes memory worse over time and separate rewriting from deletion, finding in-place
rewriting the most harmful operator; their synthetic world uses arbitrary nonce mappings, which
removes prior-fit by construction, and they explain the loss as over-generalisation. The Compaction
Cliff \citep{compactioncliff2026} measures constraint recall collapsing over five compactions but has
no prior-fit dimension. \citet{zahn2026facts} claim constraints drift toward plausible defaults after
three self-summarisations without per-item data or recorded replacement values. We supply the
manipulated variable, the replacement values, and the controls that separate rewriting from capacity.

\paragraph{Iterated learning and transmission chains.} Our framing follows
\citet{griffiths2007language} and \citet{kirby2007innateness}, with \citet{zhu2025eliciting} showing
that iterated in-context learning elicits an LLM's priors and \citet{ren2024iterated} and
\citet{cui2025sharper} questioning how closely in-context inference matches posterior sampling.
\citet{perez2025cultural} and \citet{acerbi2023large} study attractors and content biases in LLM
transmission chains of narrative text. We study memory items with an independently measured,
graded prior, and we take the absorbing-deletion correction seriously rather than claiming
convergence to the prior.

\paragraph{Knowledge conflict.} ClashEval \citep{clasheval2024}, \citet{xie2024adaptive} and related
work grade how far context deviates from a model's parametric belief and measure whether the model
adopts it --- in a single step, for real entities. \citet{kochelka2026weak} find the single-step
effect weak, which is precisely the argument for studying accumulation across a chain.

\paragraph{Marker-level drift.} \citet{belem2026may} document certainty inflation across iterated
rewriting, and related work finds hedges and quantifiers eroded in summarisation. Those are
properties of how something is said; we measure whether the specific value survives at all, and what
replaces it.

\paragraph{Homogenisation.} Knowledge collapse \citep{peterson2024knowledge} and generative
monoculture \citep{wu2024monoculture} describe homogenisation arising from training on model output.
The mechanism here is different and complementary: it is inference-time and in-context, requires no
retraining, and operates inside a single deployed assistant's memory.
