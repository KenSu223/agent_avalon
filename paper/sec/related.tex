\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Degradation in agent memory.} \citet{zhang2026faulty} measure that continual consolidation
makes memory worse over time and find in-place rewriting the most harmful operator, but their
synthetic world uses arbitrary nonce mappings, which removes prior-fit by construction. The Compaction
Cliff \citep{compactioncliff2026} measures constraint recall collapsing over five compactions with no
prior-fit dimension, and \citet{zahn2026facts} claim constraints drift toward plausible defaults
without per-item data or recorded replacement values. We supply the manipulated variable, the
replacement values, and the controls that separate rewriting from capacity.

\paragraph{Iterated learning, transmission chains and knowledge conflict.} Our framing follows
\citet{griffiths2007language} and \citet{kirby2007innateness}, with \citet{zhu2025eliciting}
eliciting LLM priors by iterated in-context learning and \citet{ren2024iterated} and
\citet{cui2025sharper} questioning how closely in-context inference matches posterior sampling;
\citet{perez2025cultural} and \citet{acerbi2023large} study attractors in transmission chains of
narrative text. ClashEval \citep{clasheval2024} and \citet{xie2024adaptive} grade how far context
deviates from a model's parametric belief, but for a single step on real entities, and
\citet{kochelka2026weak} find that single-step effect weak. \citet{belem2026may} document certainty
inflation across iterated rewriting --- a property of how something is said, where we measure whether
the value survives at all. We study memory items with an independently measured, graded prior, and we
take the absorbing-deletion correction seriously rather than claiming convergence to the prior.

\paragraph{Homogenisation.} Knowledge collapse \citep{peterson2024knowledge} and generative
monoculture \citep{wu2024monoculture} describe homogenisation arising from training on model output.
The mechanism here is different and complementary: it is inference-time and in-context, requires no
retraining, and operates inside a single deployed assistant's memory.
